# About H & H Custom Cabinetry

For more than 20 years, H & H Custom Cabinetry has helped homeowners across the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area transform kitchens, bathrooms, living spaces, and storage areas.

The team takes time to understand each client's needs, preferences, space, and budget. Personalized guidance covers layouts, materials, finishes, doors, hardware, and practical storage. Cabinetry can suit traditional, transitional, contemporary, and modern homes.
