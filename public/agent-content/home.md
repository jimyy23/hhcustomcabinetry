# H & H Custom Cabinetry

Custom cabinetry designed for your home and built for the way you live. H & H Custom Cabinetry serves homeowners across the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area (GTHA).

## What we create

- Custom kitchens planned around lifestyle, storage needs, space, style, and budget
- Bathroom vanities in a range of styles, finishes, colours, and configurations
- Made-to-measure built-ins and storage for living areas, offices, mudrooms, laundry rooms, bars, bedrooms, and more

## Our approach

For more than 20 years, H & H Custom Cabinetry has helped homeowners transform kitchens, bathrooms, living spaces, and storage areas. The team begins by listening, then provides guidance on layout, materials, finishes, doors, hardware, and practical storage.

The process includes a free consultation, design and selection, final design, and careful installation.
