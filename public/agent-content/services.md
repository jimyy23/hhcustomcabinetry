# Custom Cabinetry Services

H & H Custom Cabinetry provides personalized design guidance and custom cabinetry for homes across the Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area (GTHA).

## Custom kitchens

Kitchen cabinetry is designed around the room, the way the household cooks and gathers, storage requirements, style preferences, and budget. Guidance is available for layout, door profiles, colours, finishes, materials, hardware, and cabinet interiors.

## Bathroom vanities

Available solutions include single and double vanities, floating and wall-mounted vanities, furniture-style designs, linen storage, and custom configurations.

## Built-ins and storage

Made-to-measure cabinetry is available for entertainment areas, fireplaces, home offices, living rooms, bedrooms, bars, laundry rooms, mudrooms, and other storage needs.

## Process

1. Free consultation
2. Design and selection
3. Final design
4. Installation
